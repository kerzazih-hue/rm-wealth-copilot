# rm-wealth-copilot
Client Preparation Copilot (CRM and Portfolio)

The goal is to help Relationship Managers to save time and improve efficiency. 
Weekly Impact: For an RM preparing 8 to 10 client reviews/calls per week, this saves 5 to 6.5 hours every week (nearly one full workday).

Designed as an end-to-end copilot for Relationship Managers and Independent Asset Managers (EAMs), the tool connects two workflows:
RM Client Prep: Transforms raw CRM profiles and liquidity drag into a structured pre-call briefing, illustrative model allocation, and a compliant, personalized outreach draft.
Live Quantitative Backtest & Stress-Test: Automatically pulls that allocation into a live market engine (^SSMI / SPY) to calculate annualized return, volatility, Sharpe ratio, and max drawdown—paired with an AI macro stress-test.
Building this reinforced a clear conviction: the biggest competitive advantage in financial services comes from professionals who can translate business needs into practical AI workflows.
